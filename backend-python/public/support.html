Rebutto Support

For help, bug reports, privacy requests or offensive-content reports, email zhanghaoranmatt@gmail.com. Rebutto is operated by Haoran Zhang.

Reporting a problem

Include your app version, device model, the steps that led to the problem and the error shown. A screenshot can help, but remove private information. Never send your password.

Text PvP

Both players sign in, open PvP and use the same friend room code to match. Send at least one argument each. Both players must finish and consent before Google Gemini judges the conversation. If judging fails, use Retry AI judging. AI scores and feedback are intended for practice and may be inaccurate.

Safety

Use Leave debate to exit a room. Use Report & block to report an opponent and prevent matching with that account again. You can also email the support address with the relevant room details. Do not share sensitive personal information in debates.

Account deletion

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account. The app confirms the request and asks for your password for password-based accounts. This removes associated records from the active database. Contact support for help or questions about backups.
