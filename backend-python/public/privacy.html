Rebutto Privacy Policy

Updated October 17, 2026. Rebutto is operated by Haoran Zhang. Contact zhanghaoranmatt@gmail.com for privacy questions or support.

Information we collect

  • Account email, display name, password hash and account identifier.
  • Optional profile information you provide, such as a profile photo, location text, headline or bio. Location text is entered by you; the app does not request GPS location.
  • Placement answers, written debate arguments, PvP messages, practice results, coaching feedback, AI judgments, XP, progress and matchmaking records.
  • Abuse reports and blocking records.
  • Our server may log IP addresses, request paths, timestamps and technical errors to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

We use this information to provide sign-in, save practice and conversation history, match debate participants, synchronize messages, generate requested AI feedback, calculate progress and investigate reported abuse. Data is associated with your account where needed for these functions.

Storage and sharing

Our application backend and database are hosted on AWS. Your device stores a sign-in token and display preferences. Your PvP opponent can see your display name, assigned match details and messages in the shared room. Do not put sensitive personal information in debate messages.

Google Gemini AI

Before an AI opponent response or coaching request, the app asks permission to send debate text, the topic, chosen side and opponent character to Google Gemini. For PvP judging, both participants must consent when finishing before the complete text conversation, topic and assigned positions are sent to Gemini. A judging retry may send that conversation again.

We do not intentionally include account names, email addresses or passwords in AI prompts. Any personal information you type into a debate is nevertheless part of the text you authorize us to send. AI replies, feedback and judgments may be inaccurate.

Google processes these requests under its Gemini API terms and applicable privacy policy. Data handling can depend on the API service tier and Google's terms. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information.

Voice, photos and tracking

Typed responses are supported. Where optional browser voice recognition is available, it is handled by the browser or device speech service. Rebutto does not store audio recordings. A selected profile image may be stored with your profile. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs in this version.

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while your account exists. Use Profile → Settings → Delete account to remove your account and associated records from the active database. For a password account, the app asks for the password to confirm deletion. Backup copies and operational logs may remain until replaced or removed. Contact us for access, correction, deletion or backup-retention questions. Requests must be verified before account data is disclosed or changed.

Safety and contact

You can leave a PvP room or use Report & block to report an opponent and prevent future matching with that account. Basic text filtering is applied before messages are posted. Contact zhanghaoranmatt@gmail.com about offensive content or privacy concerns.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the service changes and will update the date on this page. Material changes to AI sharing will require appropriate notice and consent.
